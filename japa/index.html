Japa

A quiet mantra counter for Android.

What it does

  • One tap anywhere is one bead. A round is your mantra's mala, 108 beads for most.
  • Hare Krishna and many other mantras, or add your own.
  • Eyes-closed mode, and the volume keys can count, so the phone can rest in your lap.
  • Reminders and alarms for your chanting time.
  • A meditation timer and stopwatch.
  • Your record of rounds and days, with backup and restore.
  • English and Hindi, day and night themes, and a Comfort mode with larger buttons.
  • Works fully offline. No account, no sign-in.

Ads

Japa is free. A small banner appears when a round is complete and at the end of the Settings pages. Ads never appear while you chant or meditate.

You can switch ads off at any time in Settings → Support Japa. Nothing else in the app changes.
