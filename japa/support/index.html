Support Japa

Japa is free, made by one developer. A quiet banner keeps it going.

Support unlocks nothing, earns no badge and is never recorded in your practice. If Japa helps you, these help it most:

  • Rate Japa on Google Play. A few honest words help others find it.
  • Share it with someone who chants.
  • Tell me what to improve, or what to add next.

You can switch ads off at any time in Settings → Support Japa. Nothing else in the app changes.
