Japa privacy policy

Effective 27 September 2026 · Applies to the Japa app for Android (package com.tsoni.japa)

In short

  • Your practice (counts, rounds, mantras, reminders, settings) stays on your phone. Japa has no account and no server, and the developer never receives it.
  • Japa shows small banner ads from Google AdMob. To serve them, Google collects some device data, such as your IP address and advertising ID.
  • You can switch ads off in Settings → Support Japa.

Who is responsible

Japa is made by Toshik Soni, an individual developer in India ("I", "me"). Contact: tsoni.kota+apps@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Japa stores the following only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Your counts, rounds, sittings and daily totals, and your stats.
  • Mantras you add or edit, your daily goal and your reminders and alarms.
  • Your settings, including language, theme and text size.
  • The age range you pick in the intro, if you pick one. It is used only to choose the starting text size and is never sent anywhere.
  • Sounds you add yourself. Japa copies only the file you choose into its private storage.

Japa does not include analytics or crash-reporting tools, and it does not read your contacts, photos, location, microphone or other apps.

Backups

If you use Backup & restore, Japa writes a file to the place you choose and reads only the file you pick to restore. If your phone's Android backup is turned on, Android may include Japa's practice record and settings in your device backup to your Google account. That backup is handled by Google under your account and is not visible to me.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Japa is free and is supported by banner ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google. Ads appear only when a round is complete and at the end of the Settings pages, never while you chant or meditate.

When ads are on, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app automatically collects and sends to Google:

  • Your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location.
  • Device identifiers: the Android advertising ID and app set ID.
  • Interactions such as app launches, ad views and taps on ads.
  • Diagnostic information about the app's and the SDK's performance.

Google uses this data to show and measure ads, for analytics and to prevent fraud and abuse. It is encrypted in transit. Google's handling of it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Consent and personalised ads

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent message asks for your choice before any ads load. If you don't consent to personalised ads, only non-personalised ads are requested.
  • You can change your choice later in Settings → Support Japa → Privacy options. This row appears where the law requires it.
  • Everywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your phone's settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads). You can also manage Google ads at My Ad Center.

Switching ads off

Turn off Ads in Settings → Support Japa. Japa then stops requesting ads, and from the next time it opens it does not start the ads SDK at all. Nothing else in the app changes. When the app opens, it still asks Google's consent service whether a consent message applies in your region. That check sends basic technical information, such as your IP address, to Google.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show reminders, alarms and the end of a meditation timer.
  • Alarms & reminders: so reminders and alarms ring on time.
  • Full-screen alarm: so an alarm can ring over the lock screen.
  • Do Not Disturb access (optional): only if you turn on Do Not Disturb while chanting.
  • Background sound: so background sound and alarms keep playing with the screen off.
  • Start at boot: to set your reminders again after the phone restarts.
  • Vibration and keeping the screen awake: for haptics and while you count.
  • Internet, network state and advertising ID: used only by the ads SDK.

Google Play

If you install Japa from Google Play, Google's own terms and privacy policy apply to the store. If you have chosen to share usage and diagnostics with Google, I may see anonymous, aggregated crash and performance statistics in Google Play Console. These do not include your practice data.

Children

Japa is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Everything Japa stores is on your phone. Uninstalling Japa, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes it. Device backups are managed in your Google account settings. Because I hold none of your data, there is nothing for me to delete. For data collected by Google for ads, use the controls above or Google's own tools.

Security

Your data stays in Japa's private app storage, which other apps cannot read. Data sent to Google for ads is encrypted in transit.

Changes to this policy

If Japa's data practices change, I will update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches the app.

Contact
